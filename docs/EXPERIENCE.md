# A new home for old stories

## North star

A child can open a story on an inexpensive phone, comfortably read Telugu, understand an unfamiliar word, admire the original illustration, and continue offline. A parent can read aloud alongside them. A researcher can trace every correction to the exact source page.

## The reading experience to build

1. **Two linked views.** Reflowed reading for adjustable text size and spacing; original-page view for artwork and historical context. Keep the same story position when switching. Preserve source scans unchanged; deskewed or contrast-adjusted derivatives remain separate.
2. **Story-first navigation.** Browse by title, character, theme, length, year, and series. Let readers choose a five-minute story or resume a serial across issues. Human review establishes story boundaries and age suitability.
3. **Telugu at the center.** Test shaping and line breaking on real devices. Offer a licensed Telugu font, font-size and spacing controls, dark/sepia themes, and a distraction-free view. Do not assume Latin-language typography settings work for Telugu.
4. **Help without interrupting.** Tap a difficult word for a reviewed glossary entry; optionally reveal transliteration or a parallel translation. Keep every aid labeled and separate from the faithful edition. No silent modernization of plot, vocabulary, or historical content.
5. **Listen together.** Start with human narration; later evaluate Telugu speech synthesis with native speakers. Add word/phrase highlighting and EPUB media overlays only after alignment and pronunciation review. Separate original text from any dramatization.
6. **Offline and accessible.** Download EPUBs, retain reading progress locally, support keyboards and screen readers, describe illustrations editorially, and test with users. Avoid mandatory accounts. Let users opt in to cross-device sync.
7. **Trustworthy discovery.** Human-curated themes and reading paths; spoiler-free descriptions. Search can link to exact passages. Any future AI question-answering should cite the edition and abstain when unsupported, with no default upload of private books.
8. **Community restoration.** A scan/text comparison screen, uncertain-word queue, correction proposals, two-person review, version history, and visible attribution. Never modify the archival master. Save bounding boxes and source-page coordinates from OCR.
9. **Share a story responsibly.** Share stable story links and reading lists. Publish book files only from a rights-cleared catalog. Keep software licensing separate from book, art, font, narration, and translation permissions.

## Priorities

The first release should focus on faithful text, preserved illustrations, comfortable reflow, source-page links, and offline EPUB downloads. Story discovery and reviewed glossaries follow. Narration, translation, social features, and AI assistance come after restoration quality is established.

## Quality contract

Measure character error rate on native-speaker transcriptions stratified by decade and scan condition. Report reading-order failures, missing lines, illustration coverage, navigation correctness, file size, and device compatibility. Thresholds should be chosen after the pilot, not invented before measurement. Every source page must map to an output or an explicit failure. Never treat an OCR confidence score as proof of correctness.
